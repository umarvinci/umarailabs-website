## About me

Hi! I'm MD Farooque, a Senior Data Scientist based in Delhi NCR, India. I help businesses turn data and AI into systems that run reliably in production and pay for themselves.

Over the past 9+ years I've taken ML solutions from raw data all the way to production: ingesting and wrangling data at scale with Python, SQL, PySpark and BigQuery, building statistical and machine learning models, and serving them through APIs on Google Cloud, with exposure to AWS and Azure as well. I standardized my team's ML engineering practice on MLflow, CI/CD and drift monitoring, so models stay reproducible and healthy long after launch.

More recently I've focused on generative AI. I built the LLM/RAG evaluation framework that helped secure enterprise AI contracts with Stanford, MIT and Berkeley, and shipped **AI Tutor**, a production RAG service that answers questions from learners on live university courses.

I'm also IEEE-published, and I enjoy translating complex analytical findings into clear decisions for executives and clients.

??? note "What kinds of projects do you take on?"
    I focus on four areas: RAG systems and AI agents, LLM evaluation and guardrails, predictive machine learning (lead scoring, forecasting, churn, segmentation), and MLOps for getting models into production and keeping them healthy. If your problem sits somewhere between data and a business decision, it's probably a good fit.

??? note "What industries do you have experience in?"
    Most of my recent work is in EdTech and higher education, including AI products used by learners on Stanford and MIT courses. I've also built marketing and growth analytics (lead scoring, search-signal monetization, conversion optimization) and industrial computer vision systems. The methods transfer well across sectors.

??? note "Which tools and cloud platforms do you work with?"
    My primary production environment is Google Cloud (BigQuery, GCP ML tooling), and I also work with AWS and Azure. Day to day I use Python, SQL, PySpark, Databricks, LangChain, vector search, MLflow and CI/CD. I'm happy to work within your existing stack.

??? note "Can you help if we already have a RAG prototype that isn't working well?"
    Yes. That's one of the most common situations I see. The fix is usually upstream of the model: document parsing, chunking and evaluation. I start by measuring where quality is being lost, then fix the highest-leverage stage first. I wrote about this in detail in my blog post on [why parsing is the RAG layer you're underfunding](blog/posts/rag-parsing-layer.md).

??? note "How do you handle data security and confidentiality?"
    I'm happy to sign an NDA before we discuss details, and I can work inside your own cloud environment and access policies so data doesn't leave your infrastructure. For LLM projects I build in controls such as PII scrubbing before text reaches logs, embeddings or the model.
